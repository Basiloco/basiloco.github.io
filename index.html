The introduction

Six urgencies

Every one touching the others.

The exhibition is laid out as six rooms, one per urgency, arranged so that a visitor feels how each issue touches every other.

  1. Energy Transition
  2. Overconsumption
  3. Food & the Kindness Revolution
  4. Climate Social Justice
  5. Interconnectedness
  6. How Climate Change Affects Me

The map

  1. London · community

    Kindfire

    The Building Centre, Store Street · London Climate Action Week

    The exhibition. Ten days in June 2026 as the artistic heart of London Climate Action Week.

    Eighteen artists, six urgencies, a weekend of talks, workshops, panels and screenings, and a community that ate vegan for the duration. A second edition is in preparation.

    The 2026 record →The next Kindfire →London Climate Action Week →The Building Centre → Join in London →

  2. Scotland · community

    Project One Wave

    Coastal Debris Campaign · Captain Paul Watson Foundation UK

    The shoreline. A united movement tackling ocean plastic through collective action.

    Project One Wave brings together conservationists, artists, businesses and volunteers across the UK, merging the Captain Paul Watson Foundation’s debris campaigns into one wave: clean-ups, summits, direct action and creative outreach. Francesca Busca is one of its artists; her Maremnants series, made from ghost gear and marine debris, grew inside it. At Kindfire it held Ocean Dynamics & Interconnected Seas.

    Project One Wave →Captain Paul Watson Foundation UK → Join in Scotland →

  3. Nantes · community

    Les Hydronautes

    BIUTOPIC · Nantes, on the Erdre

    The river. A laboratory that floats, shared with whoever needs it.

    North of Nantes, on fifteen kilometres of Natura 2000 riverbank — herons, kingfishers, otters — a 1987 Renaud 8000 is being restored into a floating laboratory for researchers, associations and citizens to share: sampling the water, watching for pollution, counting what lives there. Its small sister, HYDRO-01, has already been to sea in the Faroe Islands. Les Hydronautes showed at Kindfire 2026.

    biutopic.com →@hydronautes → Join in Nantes →

  4. North-East Sicily · community

    MAREA

    Marine Protected Area · European Youth Marine Artivism

    The protected sea. European youth marine artivism, from a Marine Protected Area on the Sicilian coast.

    MAREA brings young artivists from across Europe to a Marine Protected Area in north-east Sicily. The Italian shore of the web.

    Join in North-East Sicily →

  5. Venice · science partner

    CNR-ISMAR

    Institute of Marine Sciences · science partner

    The science. Oceanography that leaves the journal and reaches a room full of people.

    A key partnership of the collective. Oceanographer Francesco Falcieri, researcher and operations manager at the institute, is part of the collective.
