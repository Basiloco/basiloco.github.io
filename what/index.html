Mission

Making abstract environmental problems personal.

We are an international eco-artivist collective, and we work as a bridge between understanding something and actually doing something about it.

The mission

We create transformative artistic experiences that awaken consciousness about our interconnected ecosystems and inspire meaningful action.

Marine conservation is where we start, not where we stop. From there the work widens to the whole ecological web — because ocean health cannot be separated from terrestrial environments, climate systems, or any other living thing.

We create spaces for empathy, knowledge and empowerment, which is how an abstract environmental challenge becomes tangible, personal and actionable across every sector.

Primary activities

Nine things we actually do.

  • Exhibitions

    Multi-disciplinary shows built to provoke wonder and responsibility in the same breath.

  • Dialogues

    Artist–scientist–community conversations that hold the whole ecological picture at once.

  • Workshops

    Hands-on sessions teaching sustainable practice and creative problem-solving.

  • Documentary

    Storytelling that puts a human face on environmental problems.

  • Interviews & Q&As

    Scientists, engineers and field experts, asked the questions people actually have.

  • Residencies

    Paid online residencies through IOAEA, nurturing emerging eco-artists.

  • Retreats

    Collective retreats in France and Italy for deep creative collaboration.

  • Community activation

    Connecting people to environmental opportunities where they already live.

  • Cultural interventions

    Work that shifts how people picture their relationship with nature.

Key partnerships

CNR-ISMAR Venice · IOAEA · Camden Clean Air Initiative · Marine Conservation Society · Living Streets · Blue Earth · ActForEaling · Captain Paul Watson Foundation and its Project One Wave network · The London Transport Museum · Person and Planet · BlogNotes

Our approach

Kind artivism: inclusive, open, compassionate.

Multisensory experiences that build genuine empathy between people and the natural world. Five strands, woven:

Art
as emotional connector
Science
as knowledge foundation
Community
as action network
Technology
as solution pathway
Spirit
as motivation source

Impact vision

An ecosystem of artists, scientists, engineers, businesses, communities and activists who collaborate to protect and restore the planet's health — and who find environmental action to be joyful, essential, creative work.
