The collective

Six people, four disciplines, one argument.

A mosaicist, two ocean scientists, a climate ethicist, a storyteller and a skipper. The collective is assembled the same way the work is — from pieces that were never meant to sit together.

  • Francesca Busca

    Eco-artivist, rubbish artist, waste mosaicist

    UK / Italy

    Creating artworks from waste. Founder of GREENy bastARTs, ArtforTrash, and Payment in Kind(ness). Eco-artivist teacher and speaker, using art to advocate for an all-encompassing systemic change.

    Website → ArtforTrash →

  • Joana Alarcão

    Environmental artist

    Portugal, online global presence

    Specializing in biomaterials and sustainable art practices. Founder of Insights of An Eco Artist (IOAEA).

    Website →

  • Gianluca Grimalda

    Social scientist, climatologist, activist, writer

    Italy / worldwide

    Researcher in social cohesion & climate ethics at Passau University. First employee dismissed for rejecting air travel and legally compensated for climate-based conscientious objection. His journey is documented in the film “The Researcher”.

    Wikipedia → The Researcher →

  • Elven Villecourt

    Storyteller for the ocean, CPWF content & comms manager

    France / UK

    Building knowledge and awareness through collaborative storytelling. Using visual media to inspire positive change – planting ideas like seeds, connecting people to ocean conservation through compelling narratives.

    Bio →

  • Selvaggia Bonetti

    Program & event lead

    UK / Italy

    A trained diver, skipper and ocean lover, channelling her passion for the sea and maritime decarbonisation in our collective.

    Bio →

  • Francesco M. Falcieri

    PhD marine biologist & oceanographer

    Italy

    Researcher and Operations Manager at CNR-ISMAR Venice since 2011. Using direct observations and numerical models to study the interactions between ocean physics, biology and pollution.

    Bio →
