Contact

Tell us which one you are.

Four different reasons people write to us, and four different people who answer. Picking one gets you a faster and better reply.

  • Artists

    You make work about the living world and want to show it.

    Send a link to your work, not an attachment.

    Write to us →

  • Scientists & engineers

    You have research that deserves an audience outside a journal.

    Tell us the finding and who is not hearing it.

    Write to us →

  • Venues & organisers

    You have a space, a festival or a programme and want the collective in it.

    Include dates and rough capacity if you have them.

    Write to us →

  • Press

    You are writing about the collective, Kindfire, or one of the artists.

    Deadlines get priority — say yours.

    Write to us →

Or just say hello

info@greenybastarts.net

We are a collective spread across the UK, Italy, Portugal and France, so replies come from whoever is best placed rather than fastest to the inbox.
